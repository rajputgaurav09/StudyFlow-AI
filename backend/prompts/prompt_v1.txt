Subjects: {subjects_summary}
Exam dates: {exam_dates_summary}
Available study time: {available_hours} hours daily
Priority: {priorities_summary}

Allocate time according to urgency and priority. Include specific study sessions, short breaks and revision. Do not exceed {available_hours} study hours. Present the result in a table with time, subject, task and duration. If any information is missing, state your assumptions.

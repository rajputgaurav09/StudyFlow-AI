- Subjects & Deadlines:
{subjects_json}

1. TIME BUDGET: The sum of durations for all "study", "revision", and "practice" sessions MUST NOT exceed {available_minutes} minutes.
2. BREAK POLICY: Include 10-15 minute "break" sessions between focused study blocks. Breaks DO NOT count toward the {available_hours}-hour study limit.
3. CHRONOLOGICAL CONTINUITY: Sessions must be strictly consecutive and non-overlapping. The start time of session (N) must equal the end time of session (N-1).
4. TIME MATHEMATICS: For every session, 'duration_min' must exactly equal the number of minutes between 'start' and 'end' (in 24-hour HH:MM format).
5. PEDAGOGICAL QUALITY: Break tasks into concrete, actionable study micro-tasks (e.g. "Solve 5 DP transition problems" or "Draft flashcards for Unit 2") rather than passive, vague labels.
6. EXAM URGENCY WEIGHTING: Subjects with fewer days until the exam and High priority must receive proportionally higher study minutes and early morning slots.
7. SUBJECT FIDELITY: Schedule ONLY subjects provided in the input list. Do not invent subjects.
